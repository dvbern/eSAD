Typography

Heading 1: 32px/700 - Headlines and Titles of a new page.

Subtitle 1: 24px/700 - Titles of widgets and other content windows.

Subtitle 2: 20px/700 - Used for titles in widgets.

Subtitle 3: 16px/700 - Used for lables in tables, tabs, navigation items in lists, filter titles, tertiary buttons.

Paragraph 1: 16px/400 - Mainly used for short,long texts and also regular label titles.

Paragraph 2: 12px/400 - Mainly used for teaser informations on dashboard, tags, chips etc.
